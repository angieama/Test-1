Confirm a payment

  1. Set your active fareTap Change fare. It becomes the reference for spotting underpayments and group payments.
  2. Find the customerSelect a time range, then search their first name. All amounts appear by default. Use the separate Amount paid row for underpaid, exact-fare or above-fare payments.
  3. Tick it offConfirm how many people the passenger is paying for. The row shows how many full fares are covered. Tick the receipt once for the whole group. Underpaid receipts show the balance missing and cannot be ticked as fully paid.

An extra amount does not cover another person until it reaches a full fare. For example, at KSh 100, KSh 250 covers 2 people with KSh 50 extra. This webpage uses mock payments and browser storage. Real payment confirmation and claims shared across devices require a backend connection.

Install LipaVerify

Open the public webpage on your phone, then add it to your home screen.

  1. AndroidOpen in Chrome. Use the browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone / iPadOpen in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Installation needs the hosted HTTPS webpage. This single HTML file also opens directly on your computer.
